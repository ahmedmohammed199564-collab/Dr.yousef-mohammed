FAQ

Answers to what patients ask most

And if your question isn't here, reach out and we'll answer clearly.

Is treatment painful?
We work to keep every step as comfortable as possible and use modern anesthesia techniques that minimize any discomfort. Your comfort is a priority in every session.
How long does a whitening session take?
An in-clinic whitening session usually takes about an hour, with results visible from the first session. We confirm the exact details after your examination.
Are dental implants right for me?
Implants suit most cases, but the decision depends on an examination of the mouth, bone and X-rays. We assess your case and explain your options honestly.
How often should I visit the dentist?
A check-up and cleaning every 6 months is generally recommended, and the interval can vary with your case. Regular visits protect you from bigger problems later.
What payment options are available?
We offer more than one payment method, and some treatments can be paid in installments. Contact us for the details that fit your case.
Do you handle emergencies?
Yes — we try to see emergencies as soon as possible. Call us or message us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange an urgent appointment.
